# What Is StudyVault?

**StudyVault is your own private study website.** You put your notes, documents, slides, videos and recordings into it, and it turns them into neat, readable lessons, like an online course made from your own material. It also has an **AI assistant** that answers questions using **only your notes**.

It runs on your own computer, not on a company's website, so you decide who can see it.

## What you can do with it

| You want to… | StudyVault… |
|---|---|
| Keep all your study material in one place | Accepts Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, text, images, video and audio files |
| Read your notes comfortably | Turns each file into a clean lesson page, grouped into sections like chapters of a book |
| Find something quickly | Searches every page at once |
| Ask a question about your notes | Lets you click **✦ Ask my notes**. The AI answers from your pages and shows which page each fact came from |
| Write your own notes | Lets you write new pages, with headings, lists, tables, pictures and videos |
| Share some notes with friends | Lets you add **viewers** who can read only the pages you choose |
| Study on your phone | Works in any web browser, on a phone, tablet or computer |

## Who it is for

StudyVault is for anyone who studies a lot of material and wants it **organised, searchable and private**. Students, teachers and people learning a new skill all fit.

## The two kinds of people who use it

- **Owner**: the person who runs StudyVault (you). The owner can add, edit and delete everything, and decides what is shared.
- **Viewers**: people the owner invites. They can only read pages marked **Share with viewers**. They can't see private pages, even by asking the AI.

## What makes it different from Google Drive or OneNote

- **It's yours.** Your files and the database stay on your own computer.
- **The AI only uses your notes.** It doesn't make things up from the internet. If the answer isn't in your notes, it says so.
- **It's free.** Every part used here has a free plan.
